MixMasterPro Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MixMasterPro ("MixMasterPro," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the MixMasterPro mobile app, web app, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use MixMasterPro, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Audio files you choose to load or process inside the app
  • Mix settings such as presets, EQ, volume, pan, effects, and saved sessions
  • Recent mixes and local app preferences stored on your device
  • Subscription and purchase status for Pro features
  • Basic technical information needed to run and improve the app

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide audio mixing, saving, export, and preset features
  • Remember your settings and recent mixes
  • Enable Pro features such as premium presets and studio-quality export
  • Support app performance, troubleshooting, and service improvements

3. Audio Files and Mix Content

Audio files you load into MixMasterPro are intended for your use inside the app. Saved settings and recent mixes may be stored locally on your device. Unless a feature clearly states otherwise, your audio files are not uploaded to our servers just by using the core app.

4. Radio and Online Content

MixMasterPro may provide access to internet radio stations and online music discovery sources. When you use those features, the app may connect to third-party services to stream content. Those third parties may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions and in-app purchases may be processed through Apple and related billing providers. We do not receive your full payment card details. We may receive subscription status, entitlement status, and limited transaction information needed to unlock purchased features.

6. Local Storage

MixMasterPro may store app preferences, recent mixes, preset history, and other session data locally on your device so the app can restore your workflow more quickly.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers only when needed to operate the app, process subscriptions, or support core features.

8. Data Retention

Data stored locally on your device remains there until you remove it, reset the app, or uninstall the app. Subscription and service-related records may be retained as needed for billing, legal, security, or operational purposes.

9. Your Choices

You may be able to:

  • Delete saved mixes and local app data from within the app or by uninstalling it
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple account
  • Stop using radio or online content features at any time

10. Children’s Privacy

MixMasterPro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may revise the effective date above.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@mixmasterpro.app
